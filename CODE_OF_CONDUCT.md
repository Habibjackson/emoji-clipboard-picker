# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback about code and behavior. Assume good faith, accept corrections, and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post real clipboard contents, credentials, or private messages in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository host's private contact option. If no private option is available, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without describing the incident publicly. Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation. This policy applies to project spaces and interactions made on behalf of the project.
